# Mere Kaagzaat — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 23 November 2026
**App:** Mere Kaagzaat: Family Papers (Android package `com.s0ulsh3ll.merekaagzaat`)
**Developer:** Rishabh Dahiya
**Contact:** rishabhdahiya.work@gmail.com, or [open an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/S0ul-Sh3ll/MereKaagzaat-Public/issues)

This policy explains what information Mere Kaagzaat ("the app", "we") handles, why, and who it is shared with. Mere Kaagzaat keeps a family's documents, renewals, medical records and passes on the phone.

## Summary

- You do **not** need an account. We do **not** run any servers, and we never receive your documents or any personal information.
- Your documents, photos, PDFs, medical records and everything you type are stored **only on your phone**, encrypted by default.
- Cards and photos are read and edited **on your phone**. Nothing is uploaded.
- The app shows ads from **Google AdMob**, which may collect device information such as your advertising ID and IP address.

## What stays on your phone

Documents, photos and PDFs you add; document numbers, dates, notes and tags; family profile names and relations; renewal reminders and challans; tickets, passes, gift card and voucher codes; medical records, including doctor, hospital and condition names. You can delete any of it in the app; uninstalling removes all of it.

**Health information.** Medical records you add are personal health information. They stay on your phone like everything else, are never sent to us or to advertisers, and leave the phone only when you share or back them up yourself.

**Encryption.** When encryption is on (the default), files and sensitive fields are encrypted with AES-256 (Google Tink). The key is protected by the phone's Android Keystore and cannot be copied off the phone.

**Reading cards and editing photos.** Text and barcodes are read with Google ML Kit's on-device models. The white-background tool runs an image model on the phone. The document scanner, where the phone has it, is provided by Google Play services and runs on the phone. Google may collect performance and diagnostic information about these tools, as described in [Google's ML Kit terms](https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/terms).

**Backups.** Android's cloud backup is turned off for Mere Kaagzaat. A backup is a file you choose to save (or a weekly automatic copy in a folder you pick), encrypted with your password. Without the password nobody can open it, including us. Where you keep that file is up to you.

**App lock.** Your PIN is stored as a salted, slow hash (PBKDF2) on the phone. Fingerprint or face unlock is handled by Android; the app never sees your biometric data. Screenshots and screen recording of the app are blocked by default.

## When the app goes online

**1. Ads.** A banner at the top of the main screens is served by Google AdMob. Google may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address (from which an approximate location can be derived), and information about ad views and taps, to show, measure and protect ads. Where the law requires consent (for example in the EEA and UK), you are asked first, and you can change your choice in You → Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings. See [how Google uses information from apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) and [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). Ads never see your documents.

**2. Links you open.** "Pay on Parivahan" opens the official Parivahan e-Challan website in your browser. What happens there is under that website's terms.

**3. Things you choose to send.** When you share, print or save a document, a PDF bundle or a backup, it goes to the app, printer, folder or person you pick, under their terms. Nothing is sent without your action.

## Permissions

- **Internet and network state:** ads only.
- **Notifications, exact alarms, run at startup:** renewal and pass reminders, rescheduled after a restart.
- **Biometric:** optional fingerprint or face unlock.
- **Foreground service:** switching storage between encrypted and plain.
- **Advertising ID:** used by Google AdMob as described above.

The app does not ask for your contacts, location, microphone, or access to all your files. You pick each file yourself.

## Notifications

Reminders are scheduled on your phone. When a PIN is on, reminders for locked documents show "A locked document" instead of the document's name.

## Children

Mere Kaagzaat is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them. Parents may store their children's documents in the app; those stay on the phone like everything else.

## Your choices and rights

All your data is on your device, so you can view, change, export and delete it in the app at any time. For ad data, use your phone's ad settings or the app's Ad privacy choices. Since we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to give access to or delete; if you have a question, write to us.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
